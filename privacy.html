Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Sri A to Z Mega Shopping Mall, Suryapet (since 1995) respects your privacy. This page explains what this website saves about you and why.

What we save

  • Sign-up details: your name, phone number, email and postal address, which you enter in the sign-up form.
  • Activity on the website: when you log in and log out, items you view, search, add to your wishlist, and when you tap Order now.
  • Basic device details: screen size and browser type, used to keep the site working well on all phones.

Why we save it

  • To prepare and deliver your orders and contact you about them.
  • To know which products customers like, so we can stock them better.
  • To keep the website secure and working properly.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), a secure cloud service. Only the shop owner can open and read it, using the owner login. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Orders and payments

When you tap Order now, your order message opens in WhatsApp. This website does not take card or bank details. Payments are made in the shop or as agreed with the mall.

Cookies and local storage

The site uses your browser's local storage to remember your sign-in, theme and wishlist on your device.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your details at any time by calling or messaging the mall on WhatsApp. We will remove them from our records.
